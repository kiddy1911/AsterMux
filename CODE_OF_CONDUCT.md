# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

AsterMux contributors, maintainers, and users are expected to make participation
welcoming, professional, and free from harassment. We welcome constructive
technical disagreement; personal attacks and discriminatory behavior are not
acceptable.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent while discussing technical decisions.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused on the project and reproducible facts.
- Respect privacy. Never publish another person's credentials, private logs,
  personal information, or workspace contents.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, moderation, and repository safety.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized content, deliberate
outing of private information, spam, and sustained disruption are not allowed.

## Scope

This policy applies to project-controlled spaces, including issues, pull
requests, reviews, release discussions, and other spaces where someone is
representing AsterMux.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary moderation problems, report the relevant GitHub content to the
maintainers. Do not post sensitive personal information publicly. When a private
maintainer contact is not available, use GitHub's own abuse-reporting tools.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock conversations, reject
contributions, or temporarily/permanently restrict participation when necessary
to protect the project and its community.
